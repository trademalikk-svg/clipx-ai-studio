### Article body

# YouTube Shorts Up to 3 Minutes: Eligibility, Aspect Ratio and Copyright Checks

YouTube Shorts can be longer than one minute. For standard channels, YouTube's current Help guidance says square or vertical videos up to three minutes long are categorized as Shorts when uploaded on or after 15 October 2024. That gives creators more room for a short explanation or demonstration, but duration alone is not the only thing to check.

## 1. Check the aspect ratio and duration

The video must be square or vertical to fit the standard Shorts classification described by YouTube. A wide 16:9 video is generally treated as long-form content rather than a Short. Keep the main subject visible after cropping, and review subtitles at phone size.

## 2. Upload through YouTube's supported workflow

YouTube says creators can upload Shorts through the YouTube app or YouTube Studio. Select the video, review the trim, add the title and other details, then check the audience and visibility settings before completing the upload. Platform screens and eligibility conditions can change, so use the latest official Help page for the final decision.

## 3. Understand the Content ID restriction

A Short longer than one minute with an active third-party Content ID claim is blocked globally, according to YouTube. This is a platform restriction, not an editing-app setting. Use footage and audio you own, have permission to use, or are licensed to publish. If you use a track from a platform library, check its allowed duration and usage terms.

## 4. Review the exported file before uploading

Play the exported video from beginning to end. Check that the first frame is clear, the subject stays inside the vertical crop, speech is audible, captions match the audio and names or numbers are spelled correctly. Do not assume that a successful preview guarantees a clean export on every device.

## 5. Keep the editing workflow separate from YouTube publishing

An editing tool prepares a video file; YouTube determines whether that upload is classified as a Short and whether it meets platform rules. ClipX's documented automatic duration presets are 30, 60 and 90 seconds. Do not assume that a platform's three-minute limit means every editing preset creates a three-minute clip. Check the controls and test the exported file before relying on a particular duration.

## Final checklist

- Square or vertical frame.
- Duration within YouTube's current limit.
- Audio and footage cleared for use.
- Content ID status considered, especially for Shorts over one minute.
- Captions and names proofread.
- Export played in full on a phone.
- Audience and visibility settings reviewed before upload.
