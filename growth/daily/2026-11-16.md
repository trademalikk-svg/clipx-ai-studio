**H1:** YouTube Shorts Up to 3 Minutes: What to Check Before Upload

### Introduction

YouTube Shorts can be up to three minutes long, but duration is only one part of upload eligibility. Creators should also check the video's aspect ratio, upload date, copyright status and the duration allowed for the selected music track. This guide summarizes the current official rules for standard channels and points to YouTube's Help pages for final verification.

### 1. Which videos qualify as three-minute Shorts?

For standard YouTube channels, square or vertical videos uploaded on or after 15 October 2024 can be categorized as Shorts when they are up to three minutes long. YouTube allows uploads through the YouTube app and YouTube Studio.

Official Artist Channels and channels linked to music content owners can have different date and eligibility rules. If the channel is connected to a music rights owner, check the official Help page rather than assuming the standard-channel rule applies.

### 2. Check copyright before uploading a Short over one minute

YouTube states that a Short longer than one minute with an active copyright claim of any type, including a manual claim, is blocked globally. It will not be playable or recommended while the claim remains active.

Use footage and audio that you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise properly cleared to use. If you receive a claim, review the notice and YouTube's official options. Do not assume that a claim is harmless just because the video is under three minutes.

### 3. Music duration can be shorter than the video

A three-minute video does not mean every music track can run for the full three minutes. YouTube says most songs in the Shorts Audio Library can cover up to 90 seconds in a three-minute Short, while some tracks are limited to 60 or 30 seconds. The selected audio displays its available duration.

You can also use royalty-free music from the YouTube Audio Library. Check the current track-specific rules in YouTube's music-eligibility guidance before publishing.

### 4. Plan the video before export

A longer Short should have a reason to be longer. Use a clear opening, one main explanation or story turn, and a concise ending. Remove repeated setup rather than stretching the clip to fill the time.

For a long recording, select one complete idea per clip. Keep the subject inside the vertical frame, review subtitle timing, and preview the final export. ClipX's existing workflow supports clip splitting and manual review; series setup and publishing remain in YouTube.

### 5. Final upload checklist

- Confirm the channel type and applicable eligibility date.
- Use a square or vertical aspect ratio.
- Keep the final duration within three minutes.
- Confirm that the audio and footage are cleared for use.
- Check the selected Shorts Audio Library track's displayed duration.
- Review captions, timing and framing.
- Watch the exported file before uploading.
- Recheck YouTube Help if the policy or channel type has changed.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can a standard-channel YouTube Short be three minutes?**  
Yes. Square or vertical standard-channel videos uploaded on or after 15 October 2024 can qualify as Shorts up to three minutes.

**Can a Short over one minute have an active copyright claim?**  
YouTube's current Help guidance says a Short over one minute with an active copyright claim of any type is blocked globally.

**Can every Shorts Audio Library song play for 90 seconds?**  
No. Most songs can cover up to 90 seconds in a three-minute Short, but some are limited to 60 or 30 seconds. The selected track shows its available duration.

**Does every AI-assisted edit need disclosure?**  
No. YouTube distinguishes realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content from examples such as caption creation and minor aesthetic edits. Review the official disclosure guidance for the specific content.
