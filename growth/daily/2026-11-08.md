## 6. SEO article draft

### SEO article draft — How to Resize an Existing Diwali Wishes Image for Instagram and WhatsApp

**Proposed title:** How to Resize a Diwali Wishes Image for Instagram and WhatsApp  
**Meta description:** Learn how to crop and resize an existing Diwali greeting image for social posts, keep its text inside safe margins and export a clean copy.  
**Target page / destination:** image-studio.html (existing tool; draft article only, no new public page created).  
**Primary phrase:** Diwali wishes images (seasonal trend; partial product fit).  
**Supporting phrase:** Diwali wishes in Hindi (seasonal trend; exact intent not fully known).  
**Search intent:** Seasonal image-seeking / practical image-preparation.  
**Priority:** Low; short-lived seasonal opportunity.

**Article copy:**

A Diwali greeting image may look right on your phone but become awkward when it is shared in a different social format. A square image, a tall story layout and a wide post do not use the same frame. Before sharing, check the destination format and make sure the important text and design details remain visible.

#### 1. Start with an image you can use

Choose a greeting image you created yourself or have permission to share. Keep the original file unchanged. If the image contains Hindi text, proofread the wording before resizing; cropping tools do not translate or correct the text.

#### 2. Choose the destination before cropping

Decide where the image will appear: a feed post, a story or another placement. Use the dimensions and aspect ratio shown by the destination platform at the time you post. Platform specifications can change, so do not rely on an old size chart without checking it.

#### 3. Protect the important content

Keep greetings, names, logos and decorative details away from the outer edge. A crop that looks balanced on a desktop can remove text on a smaller phone display. Preview the complete image after changing its frame.

#### 4. Crop or resize a copy

Open the existing image in SHIF-A Image Studio. The current browser tool supports local crop, resize and export. It does not create a new Diwali greeting image, generate Hindi text or automatically translate a design. Use it to prepare an image you already have.

#### 5. Review the exported file

Open the exported copy and check:
- Hindi spelling and punctuation remain readable.
- No important text or decoration has been cropped.
- The image is not stretched or distorted.
- The file opens correctly on the device you will use to post.
- The original image remains available as a backup.

#### 6. Share responsibly

Use images, fonts and artwork you own or are allowed to use. If a greeting includes another person's photo, get their permission before sharing it publicly. Add a short caption that matches the message rather than filling it with unrelated hashtags.

**Conclusion:** A few careful checks can make an existing greeting image easier to view across social formats. Start with a permitted source image, choose the destination frame, protect the text and review the exported copy. Open SHIF-A Image Studio for local crop, resize and export.
