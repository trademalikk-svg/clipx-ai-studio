### Article copy

Chhath Puja 2026 is observed from 13 to 16 November. Dated coverage identifies 15 November as Sandhya Arghya and 16 November as Usha Arghya and Parana. Local sunrise and sunset times vary, so a general article should not publish one universal timing for every city.

If you already have family footage and permission to share it, a short recap can be planned without recording anything new. Keep the edit focused on the moments that viewers can understand, and avoid exposing private details or publishing identifiable people without consent.

### 1. Start with footage you can share

Use only video already recorded safely and shared with permission. Do not enter crowded or waterside areas to capture content, and do not interrupt ceremonies. If a clip includes people who have not agreed to appear publicly, choose another clip or keep the footage private.

### 2. Choose one complete moment

A short video should have a clear beginning and ending. Select one meaningful moment rather than combining unrelated fragments. A simple structure is context, one central moment and a respectful closing message.

### 3. Split and review the source

The existing ClipX workflow supports manual or duration-based clip selection. A fixed-duration split creates sections; it does not automatically determine which moments are best. Review every section and remove parts that lack context.

See the existing guide: https://trademalikk-svg.github.io/clipx-ai-studio/split-video-into-multiple-clips.html

### 4. Check vertical framing

For a vertical Short, set the 9:16 format and inspect the preview. Keep important subjects and text within the visible frame. Check the first and last frames before exporting.

See: https://trademalikk-svg.github.io/clipx-ai-studio/guide-youtube-shorts.html

### 5. Review privacy and accuracy

Before sharing, check consent, names, location details and any private information visible in the footage. Do not add unverified ritual claims or a universal sunrise/sunset time. If using realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered footage, review YouTube's current disclosure guidance: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en

### Closing

A useful festival recap does not need complicated effects. A clear moment, accurate context, respectful editing and a careful final review are enough. Use tools only for the editing tasks they actually support, and keep private footage private.

**Editorial note:** This is a seasonal draft, not a claim that SHIF-A offers Chhath templates, automatic festival-video generation or ritual services. Review dates and local context before any publication.
