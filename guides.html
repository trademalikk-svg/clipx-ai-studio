Free Video Editing Guides for Shorts & Reels

Simple, practical walkthroughs for creators editing on Android phones and desktop browsers. Learn the workflow before you export, and understand what each browser tool can and cannot do.

ClipX runs in Free Browser Mode. Your selected source video is processed locally in your browser; cloud rendering and paid third-party AI video generation are disabled. Device memory, browser codecs and file size affect results.

Before you start

  • Use a current browser and keep enough free device memory and storage.
  • Keep an original copy of your video and images before editing.
  • Use footage, music, photos and logos you own or have permission to use.
  • Check every exported clip, subtitle and thumbnail before publishing.
  • Do not expect a tool to identify story highlights unless it explicitly says it performs semantic analysis. ClipX Automatic Shorts currently splits by time, not by story meaning.

About these tutorials

These guides describe the current public ClipX browser tools, not planned or disabled cloud features. Export options can vary by browser and device. If a step fails, try a shorter source file or another supported browser, and report the issue through Contact & Support.
