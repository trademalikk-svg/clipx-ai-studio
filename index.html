Free Online Video Editor for Shorts and Reels

ClipX AI Studio is a browser-based video editing workspace for creators who want to prepare vertical clips without installing desktop editing software. Open the site on a supported phone or computer, choose a video from your device, and use the tools that fit your task. Hindi and English creator workflows are supported.

What can you do with ClipX?

  • Trim a video: choose start and end times with the Manual Clip Cutter, preview the selected section and export it when your browser supports the source format.
  • Split a long video: use Automatic Shorts Maker to divide a selected video into sequential clips of a chosen duration. This is time-based splitting, not AI-powered highlight detection.
  • Create subtitles: write captions and export an SRT subtitle file. The on-device Whisper option can draft multilingual speech captions for supported smaller files; always review its words and timestamps.
  • Prepare creator assets: generate title and hashtag suggestions from local templates, draft video prompts and design a thumbnail using the browser canvas tool.

How to make a Short with ClipX

  1. Open ClipX and select a video stored on your device.
  2. Load the video and check that its preview plays correctly.
  3. Choose a clip duration, aspect ratio and number of clips, or set exact start and end times in Manual Clip Cutter.
  4. Create the clips, review each result and download only after checking playback and audio.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClipX a free online video editor?

The current Browser Mode provides the listed local creator tools without a subscription. Cloud rendering, paid AI video generation, live payments and premium unlocking are not active. Your device, browser and video format affect which exports work.

Does ClipX automatically find viral moments?

No. Automatic Shorts Maker currently splits a video by time intervals. It does not understand the story or select the most engaging moments. Semantic AI highlight detection is not active.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

Local preview, manual trimming and browser-based processing are designed to work on your device. The speech model and other browser libraries may download from their providers. Do not use confidential footage on a shared device, and check the privacy policy before using the service.

Can I use ClipX on Android?

You can open the website in a modern mobile browser. Large videos and on-device speech recognition need available memory, storage and processing power, so smaller test files are recommended on phones.

Feature Status & Roadmap

Browser tools included: local video preview, sequential Shorts splitting (device/browser support required), multilingual auto-captions, manual SRT subtitle builder, title/hashtag templates and video-prompt helper.

Video upload and preview work in your browser. Automatic speech recognition runs in your browser using a multilingual Whisper model; language auto-detection may make mistakes. Face detection is available as an experimental on-device test; automatic face-follow cropping, true semantic AI highlights and AI video generation are not active yet. These require extra processing or an AI service. Do not treat the activity-based highlight finder as semantic AI.
