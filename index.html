Feature Status & Roadmap

Free tools: video preview, trim/export, clip splitting, activity-based highlight suggestions, manual SRT subtitles and title/hashtag templates.

Video upload and preview work in your browser. Automatic speech recognition runs in your browser using a multilingual Whisper model; language auto-detection may make mistakes. Face detection is available as an experimental on-device test; automatic face-follow cropping, true semantic AI highlights and AI video generation are not active yet. These require extra processing or an AI service. Do not treat the activity-based highlight finder as semantic AI.
