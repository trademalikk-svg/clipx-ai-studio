How to Make YouTube Shorts from a Long Video

You can turn a long recording into shorter vertical clips by choosing useful moments, trimming them and checking each export. Long video se Shorts kaise banaye? ClipX offers local browser tools for this workflow.

Updated: 11 November 2026 · Estimated reading time: 5 minutes

1. Prepare your source video

Choose a video you own or have permission to edit. Keep the original file as a backup. For smoother editing on a phone, start with a short test file and close other heavy apps. ClipX displays a 3 GB file-selection ceiling, but that is not a promise that every phone can decode or export a file of that size.

2. Load the video in ClipX

  1. Open the ClipX Studio and sign in to your account.
  2. Open My Studio and choose a video from your device.
  3. Tap Load Video on Device and wait for the preview to appear.
  4. Confirm that the video plays and that its duration is shown correctly.

3. Choose a clip workflow

Manual Clip Cutter: enter a start time and end time in seconds to select one segment. This is useful when you already know which moment you want.

Automatic Shorts Maker: choose a clip duration (30, 60 or 90 seconds) and a number of clips (3, 5 or 10), then create the clips. This tool splits the video into consecutive time-based sections. It does not understand speech, story context or viral highlights, so review each result and rename or discard weak segments.

4. Set the vertical format and export

Choose 9:16 for a vertical Shorts/Reels layout. The export controls include a lighter 540p/24 FPS mode and a 720p/30 FPS standard mode. The lighter option may be easier for lower-powered devices, while the standard option may preserve more detail. Actual format support depends on the browser and source codec.

5. Review before publishing

  • Play the entire exported clip, not only the first few seconds.
  • Check that the important subject is visible in the vertical frame.
  • Listen for missing or distorted audio.
  • Check captions and names manually if you add subtitles.
  • Make sure the clip does not include private information or footage you cannot publish.
Privacy note: in Free Browser Mode the selected source video stays on your device for local preview/editing. Cloud video rendering is disabled. Browser libraries and speech models may still download from third-party CDNs.

Plan a YouTube Shorts series

When one topic has several useful parts, give each Short one clear job: introduce the question, explain the steps, then show an example or answer a common follow-up. Keep every episode useful on its own, use consistent naming and end with a natural pointer to the next part.

YouTube announced Shorts Series on 23 September 2026. It lets creators organize Shorts into seasons and episodes, use custom thumbnails and offer sequential playback. YouTube said the rollout was starting across web, mobile and TV; controls may vary by account. Read the official YouTube Shorts Series announcement.

Plan three connected episodes

  1. Episode 1 — the question: introduce one clear problem and tell viewers what the series will explain.
  2. Episode 2 — the useful step: explain one action or idea; keep enough context for this episode to make sense on its own.
  3. Episode 3 — the example: demonstrate the result, answer a likely follow-up and summarize the main lesson.

Use consistent episode names and a clear visual style. If Shorts Series controls are available in your YouTube Studio, organize the videos into the appropriate season and episode order, add custom thumbnails where offered and preview the viewing sequence. Keep every episode useful on its own rather than making the viewer depend on an earlier clip.

ClipX prepares individual clips; series setup and publishing happen in YouTube. To prepare a source video, read the guide to splitting a video into multiple clips.

Can a YouTube Short be up to three minutes?

For standard channels, YouTube classifies square or vertical videos up to three minutes long as Shorts when they are uploaded on or after 15 October 2024. You can upload through the YouTube app or YouTube Studio. A Short longer than one minute with an active Content ID claim of any type, including manual claims, is blocked globally on YouTube. Check that the audio and other material are cleared for use before uploading.

Official Artist Channels and channels linked to music content owners can have different date and eligibility rules. Check YouTube’s current three-minute Shorts guidance before publishing.

When a two-to-three-minute Short is useful

Use a longer Short only when the idea needs a complete setup, demonstration or story payoff. A secondary 2026 report analysing 4,615,125 videos from 25,796 channels found that the share of Shorts lasting 2–3 minutes rose from 6.1% in Q1 to 7.3% in August–October. This describes upload mix in the report’s sample; it does not prove that longer Shorts get more views, that viewers prefer them, or that every topic needs this length. Read the 2026 YouTube Trends Report. A practical structure is hook, context, one main step or story turn, then payoff; remove repeated setup instead of adding filler. When to disclose realistic AI-generated content

YouTube’s 27 May 2026 guidance says photorealistic and meaningfully altered or generated content should be disclosed. For Shorts, the disclosure appears as an on-video label; YouTube also describes automatic detection signals for some realistic AI-generated content. This is YouTube’s platform policy, not a SHIF-A feature. Review the current upload disclosure controls and read YouTube’s AI-label guidance before publishing.

For unrealistic, animated or slightly altered content, YouTube says the disclosure may appear in the expanded description. Do not assume every AI-assisted edit has the same disclosure treatment; consider how realistic and materially altered the final video is.

Common problems

Preview is black or does not load

The file codec may not be supported by your browser. Try a common MP4/H.264 source or a shorter test video.

Export is slow or stops

Large files and high-resolution exports need memory. Close other tabs, use a shorter segment and try the lighter export mode.

The automatic clips miss the best moment

That is expected: automatic splitting is based on duration, not semantic highlight detection. Use Manual Clip Cutter to select the moment yourself.
