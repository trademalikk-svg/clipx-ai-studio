Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ClipX AI Studio is an early-stage browser-based creator toolkit. This policy describes the current public website and its enabled features. Individual third-party providers may process information under their own policies.

Account and sign-in

A ClipX account is required to enter the main Studio workspace. Sign-in and account authentication are provided through Supabase. Supabase processes account identifiers and authentication data to provide this service. Do not reuse a password from another website. You can request a password reset email from the sign-in screen and set a replacement password through the verified recovery link. You can also use the account controls to sign out.

Video and text processing

In Free Browser Mode, selected video files used by local preview and browser editing tools are processed in your browser; the disabled cloud-upload control does not send the selected source video to ClipX cloud storage. Some tools may download JavaScript libraries or speech models from third-party CDNs. Model-based speech processing may run in your browser, while browser-native speech services may involve your browser or device provider. Avoid sensitive or confidential media and prompts.

Support tickets

The Contact & Support page submits support tickets to the ClipX Supabase database for signed-in users. Row-level security policies restrict ticket visibility to the ticket owner and authorized support administrators. Replies are restricted to authorized support administrators. Email notifications and live chat are not currently enabled. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials, API keys or unnecessary sensitive information in a ticket.

Local browser storage

Creator Tools may save text projects in local storage on the device and browser where they were created. These items are not a cloud backup. Clearing browser site data may remove them.

Third-party services and connection data

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads Supabase and selected JavaScript libraries or models from third-party providers. When your browser connects to those services, the providers may receive ordinary technical information such as your IP address, browser details and request metadata. Their own privacy policies and terms apply.

Microphone and speech features

Speech and voice features depend on browser and device support. If a feature requests microphone permission, the browser or its speech provider may process audio. You can deny or revoke microphone permission through your browser settings.

Free Browser Mode and disabled services

Cloud video upload/rendering and external paid AI video generation are disabled in the public Free Browser Mode. The repository may contain experimental backend code that is not exposed as an enabled public workflow. Do not assume a feature is available unless the website clearly indicates that it is enabled.

Advertising

As of the date above, the website does not have Google AdSense or other display-ad code installed. Third-party providers may still use their own technologies as described in their policies.

Data choices and contact

For account or support questions, use the signed-in Contact & Support page. No self-service account deletion portal is currently provided. Do not post private information in public GitHub discussions or issue trackers.

Children

ClipX is a development-stage project and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Younger users should use online services with appropriate parent or guardian guidance.
