Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ClipX AI Studio is an early-stage browser-based prototype. This policy describes the current intended behaviour, not a guarantee about every browser or third-party service.

Information you enter

Video files selected for local preview and editing are processed in your browser by the available client-side tools. Do not upload or enter sensitive, confidential or personal information.

Account sign-in

The website uses Supabase authentication for account sign-in. Account information is handled by Supabase under its own privacy and security terms. Do not use an account password that you use elsewhere.

Local storage

The Creator Tools page may save text projects in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing browser site data may remove them. These saved items are not a cloud backup.

Third-party services

The site loads libraries and services from third-party providers, including GitHub Pages hosting, Supabase and JavaScript package CDNs. When your browser connects to these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and request information. Their own privacy policies and terms apply.

Browser permissions and speech tools

Speech-to-text and voice features depend on browser and device support. If you grant microphone permission, the browser may process audio using its own speech service; processing may not remain entirely on your device. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in your browser settings.

AI and support

Runway AI video generation is an experimental feature. If the required server-side Supabase Edge Function is deployed and enabled, your prompt is sent to that function and then to Runway. Prompts and generated media may be processed by those providers under their own terms. Generation may consume paid provider credits. This feature has not completed end-to-end production testing; do not submit sensitive or confidential prompts. The Contact page links to GitHub Issues; if you choose to post there, your report may be public and is handled by GitHub under its policies. The Contact page itself does not collect or send form submissions.

Advertising and cookies

As of the date below, ClipX AI Studio does not have Google AdSense or other display-ad code installed. If advertising is added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising partners, cookies or similar technologies, and available choices before ads are enabled. Third-party providers may use their own technologies as described in their policies.

Data choices and security

Text projects saved by Creator Tools are stored in local storage in the browser on the device where they were created. You can remove them using the page's delete controls or clear the site's browser storage. Clearing browser storage may also remove other site preferences. Account-related data, if account sign-in is used, is handled by Supabase. Do not enter passwords, API keys, OTPs or sensitive personal information into public support reports.

Children and contact

This project is not designed to collect personal information from children. Children should use the service with a parent or guardian's guidance. No private support inbox or dedicated data deletion portal is currently configured. For general bug reports, use the public GitHub issue tracker linked on the Contact page, but never post personal or confidential information there.
