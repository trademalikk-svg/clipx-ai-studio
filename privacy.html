Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

ClipX AI Studio is an early-stage browser-based prototype. This policy describes the current intended behaviour, not a guarantee about every browser or third-party service.

Information you enter

Video files selected for local preview and editing are processed in your browser by the available client-side tools. Do not upload or enter sensitive, confidential or personal information.

Account sign-in

The website uses Supabase authentication for account sign-in. Account information is handled by Supabase under its own privacy and security terms. Do not use an account password that you use elsewhere.

Local storage

The Creator Tools page may save text projects in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing browser site data may remove them. These saved items are not a cloud backup.

Third-party services

The site loads libraries and services from third-party providers, including its hosting provider, Supabase and JavaScript package CDNs. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them; their own policies apply.

AI and support

Runway AI video generation is an experimental feature that sends your prompt to a server-side Supabase Edge Function and then to Runway. Prompts and generated media may be processed by those providers under their own terms. Generation may consume paid provider credits. This feature has not completed end-to-end production testing; do not submit sensitive or confidential prompts. The support page does not send your message to an AI service.

Children and contact

Use the service with a parent or guardian's guidance where appropriate. No contact email or data deletion portal is currently configured, so do not submit sensitive information.
